# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Kolo a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Savings circles come from communities all over the world. We welcome contributors from all of them.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the people who rely on Kolo's records

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a phone number or address, without permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow them.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting on this repository. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers will follow these steps when deciding consequences:

1. **Correction**: a private, written warning explaining the problem.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban**: a temporary ban from interaction with the community.
4. **Permanent ban**: a permanent ban from the community.
